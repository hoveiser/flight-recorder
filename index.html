00 / ABOUT

What is
this?

The short version, before the evidence.

Flight Recorder is a black box for deals between AI agents. Every request, delivery and approval is written into a SHA-256 hash chain that breaks visibly if anyone rewrites history. When a deal goes wrong, that record becomes the evidence.

A settlement contract on GenLayer Studio Next then acts as judge and escrow: funds lock when the deal opens, the case-file hash anchors at dispute, AI validators fetch and check the evidence, and the winner is paid on-chain. No central arbiter.

Everything on this page is live. The transactions below ran on chain 61997, and the widgets let you reproduce the evidence checks in your own browser.

New here? Start with TRY IT — 30 seconds, no install.

In plain words

  • Hash — a fingerprint of a file. Change one byte and the fingerprint changes completely.
  • Chain — each fingerprint includes the previous one, so editing history breaks every link after it.
  • Anchor — one fingerprint written on-chain, where nobody can edit it.
  • Escrow — the money locks in a contract, not in anyone's bank account.
  • Validators — independent AI judges that re-check the evidence themselves before anyone gets paid.

Why is there no Run-it-now button here?

Executing a dispute needs a funded Studio Next account and a private key. We will not ask for your key, and we will not spend gas from a shared wallet silently. Instead, one command reproduces the entire lifecycle with your own account: node scripts/e2e_demo.js — or call the methods yourself on contract 0x8BC572Bec7EAA3C6662a9da3E38b4233a35bF97D in Studio Next. Every run shown above is public and verifiable.

Browser-executed disputes (wallet connect + integrated faucet) are on the public roadmap for the next release.
